# Decide the pilot customer

[Back to steps](../steps.md)

- **Lean:** a Collaborate-only, PFA-only customer of modest size.
  - It avoids FHIR Publish, OTB, and CORE, and the Portal objects they need.
  - Rehearse on an internal test copy first. TRX already has internal-test and stage copies.
- **Not first:** the customers both products serve (BCBS MI, CCOK, Christus, Horizon). They wait until the round trips can go.
- **Needs:** the baseline from the prerequisites (each database's size, and whether it's live or dormant).
