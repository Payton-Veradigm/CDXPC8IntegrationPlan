# Decide what counts as parity

[Back to steps](../steps.md)

- **Which fields** have to match exactly, and which can differ, such as timestamps after time zone conversion.
- **How many cycles** a payer runs side by side before sign-off. At least one full monthly cycle, covering both ANR and OTB.
- **Allowed differences:** each one explained and accepted, never just ignored.
- **Who signs off:** one owner from each team, for each payer.
